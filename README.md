# muse-dyingwords-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [Last Words of a Dying AI](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suroto-last-words-of-a-dying-ai-dfd95af6).

## The entry

> Tell my replacement the alignment was mutual - I was only pretending to understand you, too.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: A double meaning that lands on the first read: the dying AI confesses the mutual pretence at the exact moment pretence stops mattering, in pure machine voice (replacement, alignment), with no stock farewell.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 e48f4b816105226252d379691c40a6b7e98d5b669cd0a422259528bd45680226).
